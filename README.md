## The entry

> A traffic jam is a school hallway when the bell rings, with a hundred cars all trying to squeeze through one door at once.
>
> It maps how too many things pushing through one narrow opening all slow each other down, but it leaves out that cars cannot squeeze sideways the way children can, and that a jam is often caused by a crash far ahead that nobody in the queue can even see.

Two lines explaining a traffic jam to a 10-year-old: line 1 is one vivid sentence built on a single analogy (a school hallway when the bell rings), and line 2 is one sentence on what the analogy maps and what it leaves out. Total 377 characters including the newline, under the 400-character gate.

## Run it

```bash
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` and prints them.
